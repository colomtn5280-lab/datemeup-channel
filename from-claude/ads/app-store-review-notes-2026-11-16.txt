1) PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
DateMeUp helps couples and friend groups decide what to do. Members add ideas (restaurants, events, activities), everyone votes I'm in / Maybe / Not for me, and when everyone agrees the plan locks in and can be added to the calendar. Audience: adults planning date nights and group outings. It replaces the "what do you want to do?" back-and-forth in group chats.

2) HOW TO TEST
Demo account: review@datemeup.app (password in Sign-In Information). It's in the group "Review Crew" (3 members):
- "Weekend plans" (open): tap an option and vote to see live vote counts.
- "Friday night out" (decided): a locked plan with everyone's votes.
- Create a plan: Plans > group > New proposal > Add another activity (Discover) > Send.
- Sign-in options: email, Sign in with Apple, or Google.

3) ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile > Account > Delete account > type DELETE > Delete account. This permanently removes the account, groups owned and votes.

4) USER CONTENT, REPORTING AND BLOCKING
User content is text only: group names, plan titles, notes, discussion comments and custom After Dark ideas. On a group page, tap ••• next to a member > Report User or Block User. Group owners can also Remove from group. Reports go to support@datemeup.app and are reviewed within 24 hours.

5) AFTER DARK
An optional section with romantic and playful ideas for couples (e.g. "Truth or dare for couples", "36 questions that lead to love") plus late-night venues and events. No nudity, no sexual content, no user-uploaded media.

6) ADS AND TRACKING
The app shows Google AdMob ads (banner and interstitial). On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt appears after a short explainer, and only personalized ads use tracking. EU/UK users get a Google consent form, and Profile has a Privacy choices option.

7) LOCATION
Approximate, when-in-use only, to suggest nearby ideas in Discover. The app works without location (users can type a city).

8) EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Backend: Google Cloud Run, PostgreSQL
- Sign-in: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In
- Places and photos: Google Maps Places API, Foursquare
- Events: Ticketmaster, Eventbrite, PredictHQ, Bandsintown
- Web search and idea suggestions: Tavily, Firecrawl, Google Gemini
- Email: Resend
- Push notifications: Expo / Apple Push Notification service
- Ads: Google AdMob (UMP consent)
- Payments: none in the app (no in-app purchases or paid content)

9) REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. Event and venue suggestions depend on the user's area. EU/UK/Switzerland users see the GDPR ad-consent form; some US states get an opt-out option.

10) REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. Venue and event data come from the licensed APIs above and link to the original sources.

11) SCREEN RECORDING
Attached: recorded on a physical iPhone. It shows launch, sign-up/login, creating and voting on a plan, the match, reporting and blocking a user, and account deletion.
